# Project Brief — `jbr-factory`

## Why this exists
Small firms book shared rooms, cars and people by e-mail and spreadsheets. Double
bookings and "who has the van on Thursday" cost an hour a day per office manager.

## Who it is for
Office managers at 5–50 person firms who own the calendar, and their colleagues who
book. Not for enterprises with a facilities department; not a consumer app.

## What "good" means
- A booking takes under 30 seconds end to end.
- Zero double bookings once a resource is in the system.
- Office managers stop keeping a parallel spreadsheet within a month.

## What it is — and is not
A calm booking system for shared resources with national-id login. It will never be a
project-management tool, a chat, or a marketplace.

## Constraints that shape everything
Norwegian market first (Vipps login, NOK, GDPR); one small team; boring stack.

## How we work here
Small features, shipped often. Boring code wins. Nothing merges with red CI.
