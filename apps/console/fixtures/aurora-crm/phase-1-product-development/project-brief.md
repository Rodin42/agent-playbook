# Project Brief — `<product name>`

> Written **before anything else**, by the product owner or the operator, in plain
> words. The strategists read it before every shaping pass; the twin reads it before
> every masterplan. `feature-plan.md` answers *what next* — this answers *why at all*.
> Keep it under a page. Revisit it when the product changes direction, not per feature.

## Why this exists
The problem in the world this product removes, and for whom. Not features — the pain.

## Who it is for
The users and buyers, in one or two lines each. Who it is explicitly NOT for.

## What "good" means
How we will know the product works: the 2–4 outcomes that matter, stated so Phase 3
could one day measure them.

## What it is — and is not
The shape of the product in a paragraph, and the standing non-goals: the things this
product will deliberately never do (feature-level out-of-scope lives per entry; this is
the product-level line).

## Constraints that shape everything
Budget, timeline, compliance, brand, platform — the standing constraints every feature
inherits without restating them.

## How we work here
Anything about pace, quality bar, or taste that a stranger (or an agent) should know
before proposing work — one short list.
